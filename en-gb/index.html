FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I know if I snore at night?

Place your iPhone beside the bed and start Go Sleep. It saves only likely snoring events as short clips you can replay in the morning.

Can it record sleep talking?

Yes. Speech-like sounds that may be sleep talking are detected on device and labelled separately from snores and coughs.

Will it fill my phone with audio files?

No. Silence is never intentionally stored—only short clips of detected events, and you can delete everything in-app.

Is Go Sleep a medical device?

No. Snore Load and nightly trends are wellness information, not a medical diagnosis.
